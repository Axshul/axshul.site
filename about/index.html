01 The story
Origin storyWhere it started

My path into AI and workflow automation was sparked by watching my brother Deepankar Bhadrasen, currently the Founding AI Engineer at TrueHorizonAI. Seeing the systems he was building pushed me to find out what is really possible with autonomous AI agents.

I don't just build scripts, I architect entire ecosystems. I've deployed multiple complex n8n automation projects that handle everything from data orchestration to autonomous AI decision-making.

ExperienceRoles that shaped me

I've had the privilege of holding high-impact roles. I worked with FRVR as their Technical Architect, where I helped shape the structural integrity of their platforms. Before that I served as the Principal Software Engineer for the BlueWizard Digital project, leading engineering for premium digital experiences.

Most recently I co-founded Qixazow.com, a joint venture with two fellow AI professionals. Qixazow is a publication and AI consultancy for people who want to stay ahead of the curve: in-depth articles and video content on n8n, Claude and the tools shaping the future of AI.

Community & contract workWhat I do daily

I serve as a Community Moderator for the n8n community and I'm the top contributor on the forum, helping developers master complex automation workflows every day.

As an AI automation specialist I also take on contract work: designing and deploying custom AI-powered workflows, intelligent agents and end-to-end automation systems for businesses. If you need an automation architect who ships production-grade solutions, let's talk.

I document my technical deep-dives and architectural insights in the Guide and on the blog, to lift the next generation of engineers.
