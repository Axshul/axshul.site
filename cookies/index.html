COOKIES

Cookiesaxshul.site

No cookies, honestly.

axshul.site sets no cookies. Here is everything that touches your browser storage, and every third-party request a page makes.

Last updated: 24 September 2026Operator: Anshul Namdev, IndiaContact: sales@axshul.site

The short version

axshul.site sets no cookies. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for preferences. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to. What follows is the complete list of everything that touches your browser storage, plus the third-party requests that could set cookies on their own domains.

Storage we use on your device

NameTypeSet byPurposeExpires
hivesessionStorageaxshul.siteRemembers, for the current tab only, that the intro animation has played so it is not repeated on every pageWhen the tab closes
notes datalocalStorageaxshul.site (/Notes/)Saves the notes you write in the tool on your own device; nothing is uploadedUntil you clear it

sessionStorage and localStorage are not cookies: they are never sent to our server, and they cannot be read by other websites. Clearing site data in your browser removes both.

Some page assets are loaded from other companies' servers. We do not set cookies through them, but their servers receive your IP address and may apply their own cookie practices on their domains.

Managing storage

You can block or clear storage for axshul.site in your browser settings at any time. The site keeps working without it: the only effect is that the intro animation plays again and notes in /Notes/ are not kept between visits.

If this changes

Should we ever add a tool that sets cookies, for example an analytics service or an embedded scheduling widget, we will add a consent banner first and update this page and the privacy policy. The date at the top of each page tells you when it last changed.
