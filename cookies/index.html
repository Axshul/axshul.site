Cookies, explained.

Browser storage for tools, third-party assets and archived forum pages on axshul.site.

Last updated: 15 October 2026Operator: Anshul Namdev, IndiaContact: sales@axshul.site

The short version

The portfolio and tools use browser storage rather than our own cookies. The table below explains that storage and the third-party requests made by embedded content. Third-party providers may apply their own cookie practices.

Storage we use on your device

NameTypeSet byPurposeExpires
notes datalocalStorageaxshul.site (/Notes/)Saves the notes you write in the tool on your own device; nothing is uploadedUntil you clear it
galaxyThoughtsIndexedDB, axshul-galaxy databaseaxshul.site (/Galaxy/)Saves your thoughts on your own device; nothing is uploadedUntil you delete them or clear site data

localStorage and IndexedDB are not cookies: their contents are not sent to our server, and they cannot be read by other websites. Clearing site data in your browser removes them. The portfolio does not store a flag for its animations.

Third-party requests

Some page assets are loaded from other companies' servers. We do not set cookies through them, but their servers receive your IP address and may apply their own cookie practices on their domains.

The three archived community directories use only this site's own assets. They contain public links to community.n8n.io and do not load the original forum scripts, account data or tracking code.

Managing storage

You can block or clear storage for axshul.site in your browser settings at any time. The site keeps working without it: notes in /Notes/ and thoughts in /Galaxy/ will not be kept between visits. Portfolio animations do not use browser storage.

If this changes

Should we ever add a tool that sets cookies, for example an analytics service or an embedded scheduling widget, we will add a consent banner first and update this page and the privacy policy. The date at the top of each page tells you when it last changed.
